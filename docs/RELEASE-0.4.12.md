# Contraband Cases 0.4.12 — Compact prizes and Messenger delivery

For **SPT 4.1.5**. Update the client, server and shipped reward packs together.

## Changes

- Replaces fresh bulk cargo draws with **117 eligible compact rewards** across
  17 packs: raid kits, specialist gear, compatible weapon/ammunition combinations
  and collectible sets. Value comes from useful items, not piles of medicine.
- Delivers opening rewards, premium rewards, Cash Cache and Relay cash-outs
  through **Mechanic's Messenger**. Collect attachments individually as stash
  space allows; no automatic payout insertion into the stash or sorting table.
- Splits large payouts into messages with at most eight separate item roots,
  preserving their exact quantities and assembled attachments. No mandatory
  backpack/container wrapper is added. Uncollected attachments last ten years.
- Preserves all 252 published cargo definitions for existing claims and recovery.
  Already-paid inventory prizes are not moved or mailed again. Unpaid legacy
  payouts migrate only with explicit proof that they were never delivered.
- Adds save-file verification, failure quarantine and retry protection to avoid
  duplicate or falsely acknowledged rewards after interrupted/failed saves.
- Clarifies Send to Messenger, Retry Delivery, Sent to Messenger and Save & Close.
  Resuming a pending reward does not reroll it. Collected/deleted mail attachments
  are never regenerated; deleting mail discards its uncollected contents.
- Blocks autogenerated fake-player case/key flea offers and generated barter
  requirements. Removes stale owned fake-player offers and refreshes cached
  Mechanic prices. Real-player/unknown-origin/unrelated offers are preserved.

Current cargo limits are eight separate roots, 128 total item/attachment nodes and
64 storage cells. Final all-pack projections reach at most 8 roots / 36 nodes /
52 cells. Larger historical and currency payouts retain their original amounts.
MCM case/key testing grants remain direct inventory grants, not reward payouts.

## Economy

Automatic case prices still follow the installed catalog and existing settings.
The final all-pack offline projection gives Mixed **1,142,000**, Operations
**1,247,000**, Relics **1,004,000** and Black Site **1,085,000 RUB**. The native Cash
Cache projection is **1,018,000 RUB**. These are not fixed prices or guaranteed
trader profits; installed mod hooks and configuration can change them.

Losses, near-even results, normal wins and rare chase prizes remain. Epic 1.5%
and Legendary 0.2% surprise openings are retained. Cash payouts and probabilities
are unchanged, including 4 Bitcoins at 0.90%, 10 at 0.10%, 80 GP Coins at 3% and
200 at 1%. Detailed assumptions and outcome distributions are in README.md.

Universal find-only keys, crate-only case loot, 2.2/1.1% added key/case table
weights, 5 kg cases, spinner behavior and accepted Unity models/sounds are unchanged.
Optional integrations still require their own compatible mods. No third-party
mods, captured definitions or editable licensed artwork are redistributed.

## Install and rollback warning

Download `ContrabandCases-0.4.12-SPT4.1.5.zip` and its SHA256 manifest. GitHub's
automatic source archives are not installable packages.

1. Close the game, launcher and server. Back up the client plugin, server mod,
   settings, `SPT_Runtime/user/profiles` and `SPT_Runtime/user/profileData` together.
2. Extract the install ZIP into the SPT game root, updating both DLL sets and
   shipped reward packs. Keep `SPT_Runtime/user/mods/Wade-ContrabandCases` unchanged.
   Preserve your existing config and any custom reward packs; review edits to
   shipped packs before replacing them.
3. Start the server/game yourself when ready. Verify prices and opening odds,
   then test Messenger delivery and partial collection on a backed-up test save.

Delivery records now use schema 8. **After using this version, do not downgrade
only the DLLs.** Any rollback requires a coherent matching backup of both mod files
and profile/delivery state, with the game/server closed. Never mix an older journal
with a newer profile or vice versa. Do not blindly restore backups over new progress.
